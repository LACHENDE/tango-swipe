How it works

Both players tap Ready to open their microphones. After 3, 2, 1, read the hiragana. Short, beginner words only; no kanji or translation questions.

Recognition time includes your browser’s processing delay. Readings within 0.3 seconds tie. This is a friendly prototype, not a pronunciation test.

Microphones stop after each word. Your browser may send audio to its speech service. This game sends only round timing and scores to the other player.

A friend needs access to this site. Earphones and separate spaces help keep your voices apart.
