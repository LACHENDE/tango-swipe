The invite keeps you on the same connection. If a room won’t connect, try the other route together.

How do we play?

You each get a different word. Read it, remember it, then tap its kana — or say it into the mic. Both words complete one bridge. Eight bridges get Tetsu home!

Finish early? Send your friend a hint. After four bridges, choose a safe path or a shorter timer for double snacks. You share three hearts, so help each other.

Voice is optional. Your browser may use an online speech service. Mic audio and transcripts are not sent to your friend. You can keep playing with kana tiles if voice doesn’t work.
